# EPA: UV Index Scale
Source: https://www.epa.gov/sunsafety/uv-index-scale-0

| UV Index | Risk Level | Recommended Precautions |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Low | No protection needed for most people; minimal sun protection for extended time outside. |
| 3-7 | Moderate to High | Seek shade during late morning through mid-afternoon. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 15+ generously; wear a hat, protective clothing, sunglasses. |
| 8+ | Very High to Extreme | Extra caution: minimize time outside during late morning through mid-afternoon. Seek shade whenever your shadow is shorter than your height. Apply broad-spectrum SPF 15+ (higher preferred) and wear full protective gear. |

## Shadow Rule
If your shadow is longer than you are tall (early morning / late afternoon), UV exposure is lower. If your shadow is shorter than you are tall (around midday), UV exposure is higher — seek shade.
