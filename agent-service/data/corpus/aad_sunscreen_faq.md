# AAD: Sunscreen FAQs
Source: https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/sunscreen-patients/sunscreen-faqs

## What Sunscreen Should I Use?
Broad-spectrum (UVA + UVB), SPF 30 or higher, water resistant.

## How Much to Apply
Most adults need about 1 ounce (a shot glass full) to cover uncovered skin. For the face, at least 1 teaspoon. Don't forget tops of feet, ears, neck, top of head. Apply 15 minutes before going outdoors.

## How Often to Reapply
Reapply approximately every 2 hours when outdoors, or immediately after swimming or heavy sweating, per the bottle's directions.

## Broad Spectrum
UVA rays prematurely age skin (wrinkles, age spots) and pass through window glass. UVB rays are the primary cause of sunburn and are blocked by window glass. Broad-spectrum products protect against both.

## Water Resistance
No sunscreen is "waterproof" or "sweatproof" — always reapply after swimming or sweating.

## Expiration
FDA requires sunscreen to remain effective for at least 3 years. Check expiration dates; discard expired product; avoid storing in direct sun/heat.
